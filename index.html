The problem

Campaigns ship. Pipeline leaks.

Most B2B teams produce plenty of marketing. The leaks are in the joins between requests, production and revenue.

Leak A · Intake

Requests arrive from everywhere

Sales, product and leadership all ask in different places. Nothing is prioritised against pipeline, so the loudest request wins.

Leak B · Production

Every campaign starts from zero

No templates, no QA checklist, no reusable copy. A single email takes days and quality depends on who built it.

Leak C · Measurement

Reporting stops at opens and clicks

Nobody can say which programs created opportunities, so budget follows opinion instead of revenue.
