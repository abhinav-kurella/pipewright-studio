Findings

Five leaks, ranked by revenue impact.

Leak A · Lead scoring rewards activity, not fit

High
What we found

Scores come from email opens and page views only. 41% of Q3 MQLs were students, job seekers or companies under 10 staff.

Why it matters

Sales stops trusting MQLs, follow-up slows down, and real buyers wait in the same queue as poor-fit leads.

Fix

A two-part score: fit (industry, size, role) plus intent (pricing page, demo page, repeat visits). An MQL needs both.

Effort · 1 weekOwner · Marketing ops

Leak B · No agreed handoff between marketing and sales

High
What we found

MQLs land in a shared queue with no owner. Median first response is 31 hours, and 18% got no response at all.

Why it matters

Interest fades quickly after a buyer raises a hand. Every unworked MQL is paid-for demand thrown away.

Fix

Round-robin routing, a same-day response target, and a recycle path back into nurture for leads sales rejects.

Effort · 1 weekOwner · Marketing + sales lead

Leak C · Campaign source is not captured

High
What we found

UTM tags are used inconsistently and the opportunity record has no required source field. 78% of opportunities show "Direct" or blank.

Why it matters

Nobody can show which programs create pipeline, so budget follows opinion.

Fix

A UTM naming standard, a required source field on opportunities, and a weekly campaign-to-pipeline dashboard.

Effort · 2 weeksOwner · Marketing ops + CRM admin

Leak D · One generic nurture for every lead

Medium
What we found

All leads get the same monthly newsletter, whatever their role or stage. Click rate is 1.4%.

Why it matters

Ops managers and finance buyers care about different outcomes. Generic content trains people to ignore you.

Fix

Three short nurture tracks by persona, each ending in a clear next step such as a demo or a cost calculator.

Effort · 3 weeksOwner · Content + marketing ops

Leak E · Every campaign is built from scratch

Medium
What we found

No email or landing page templates, no QA checklist, and four rounds of review. A standard campaign takes 9 working days.

Why it matters

A 3-person team ships fewer campaigns, and quality depends on who built each one.

Fix

A reusable template library, an AI-assisted first draft trained on brand voice, one review step and a 10-point QA list.

Effort · 2 weeksOwner · Marketing team

Sources and method
  1. MQL → SQL median of ~13% for B2B SaaS, as cited from First Page Sage benchmark data and Salesforce State of Sales (2025) in Flighted and Breadcrumbs. Other vendors report 18–40% depending on definitions, so 13% is the conservative reference.
  2. B2B SaaS email click rate of ~3.2% from Folderly, Email Metrics Benchmarks 2025. Click rate is used instead of open rate because Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens.
  3. SQL → opportunity of ~42% from a vendor-published B2B SaaS funnel table in Understory. Methodology is not published, so this is directional only.
  4. Health score: six checks (lead quality, handoff speed, conversion, attribution, nurture engagement, production speed), each scored 0–100 and weighted by revenue impact.
